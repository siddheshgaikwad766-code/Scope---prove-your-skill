Micro-internships that prove your skills. Show startups what you can do.

SCOPE is a free-pilot platform in India for micro-internships and skill-based hiring. Students complete small, real tasks for startups, build verified proof of skill, and let startups see what they can do and internships.

For students

Swipe through real startup tasks, ship the work, and earn credentials and proof points that show what you can do. Stay anonymous until a startup picks you for a task.

How it works

  1. Create a student or startup account.
  2. Startups post tasks. Students apply to the ones that match their skills.
  3. Startups hire from a top-3 shortlist and fund escrow.
  4. Work is delivered in two milestones and signed off.
  5. Students receive the payout (simulated during the pilot), earn a credential and build proof of skill.

Frequently asked questions

What is SCOPE?

SCOPE is a platform where students complete small, real tasks for startups, build verified proof of skill, and let startups see what they can do. Startups find talent through proven work instead of resumes.

What is a micro-internship?

A micro-internship is a short, project-based assignment, usually a few hours to a few weeks, where a student completes a real piece of work for an organisation. On SCOPE, each startup task is a micro-internship that ends in a signed-off result you can show to employers.

What is skill-based hiring?

Skill-based hiring means choosing people for what they can demonstrate, such as finished work and verified skills, instead of their degree, college name or resume. SCOPE supports it by letting startups review proof of skill and hire from an anonymous shortlist.

Is SCOPE free?

Yes. SCOPE is in a free pilot. It costs students nothing. Startups can post free tasks at no cost, and funded tasks carry an 8% service fee. The hiring fee for a full-time hire is waived during the pilot.

Do students get paid?

Tasks can carry an optional bounty that the startup funds into escrow. It is released in two milestones, 30% at kickoff and 70% at final delivery, after the startup signs off the work. In the current pilot, payments are simulated and no real money moves yet.

Can startups see my name before they pick me for a task?

No. Startups see a shortlist with your year, branch, skills, proof points and match score. Your name is revealed only after a startup picks you for their task.
